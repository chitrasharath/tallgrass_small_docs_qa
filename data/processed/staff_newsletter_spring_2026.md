## New clock-in app starts in March (page 1)

Paper timesheets are going away. The new VisitTrack app replaces them for every caregiver, and it goes live on Monday, March 9. You will use it to clock in, clock out, and write your visit notes.

We know a change like this takes time. Paper timesheets will still be accepted through Friday, March 27, which gives everyone a three-week grace period. After that date, payroll can only pay hours that are in the app.

Hands-on practice sessions are held at the Cedar Street office every Tuesday at 9:00 a.m. and every Thursday at 5:30 p.m. during February. Each session lasts about 45 minutes. Bring your own phone.

If the app will not install on your phone, call Dana Whitlock in the office at extension 214. The office has six loaner phones for caregivers who need one.

## Caregiver of the Quarter (page 1)

Congratulations to Rosa Delgado, our Caregiver of the Quarter. Rosa has been with Tallgrass for six years and works mostly overnight shifts. Three families wrote in to thank her this winter, and one sent a photo album. She receives a $250 gift card and a reserved parking spot for the next three months.

## Training calendar (page 1)

CPR recertification will be held on Tuesday, April 14 at the Cedar Street office. The dementia care workshop is on Wednesday, May 6 and counts for 3 in-service hours toward your yearly total.

## Referral bonus goes up (page 1)

Know someone who would make a great caregiver? The referral bonus has doubled, from $150 to $300. It is paid after the new hire completes 90 days of work. Ask the front desk for a referral card.
