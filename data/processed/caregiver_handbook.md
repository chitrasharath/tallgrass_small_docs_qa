## Tallgrass Home Care - Caregiver Handbook (page 1)

Internal document. Tallgrass Home Care is a fictional agency used for practice data.

Last updated: January 2026

## Scheduling and call-outs (page 1)

Your schedule is published every Thursday for the following week. Check it in the mobile app before your first shift.

If you cannot work a shift, call the scheduling line at 555-0142 at least 4 hours before the shift starts. A text message to your supervisor does not count as notice.

More than 3 unexcused call-outs in a rolling 90-day period results in a written warning. A second written warning within 12 months leads to a meeting with the Care Manager.

## Clocking in and out (page 2)

Clock in with the mobile app when you arrive at the client's home. You must clock in within 7 minutes of the scheduled start time. The app checks your location, and you must be within 150 meters of the client's address for the clock-in to be accepted.

If you forget to clock out, tell your supervisor the same day. A missed clock-out must be corrected by a supervisor within 24 hours, or the visit cannot be billed.

## Clocking in and out > Visit notes (page 2)

Complete your visit notes before you leave the home whenever possible. Notes must be submitted no later than 2 hours after the visit ends. Write what you did and what you observed. Do not write opinions about the client or the family.

## Pay > Overtime (page 3)

The workweek runs from Sunday to Saturday. Hours over 40 in one workweek are paid at 1.5 times your regular rate. All overtime needs approval from the Care Manager before you work it.

## Pay > Mileage (page 3)

Driving between client homes during the day is reimbursed at $0.67 per mile. The drive from your own home to your first client, and from your last client back home, is not reimbursed. Submit your mileage log by the 5th of the following month.

## Pay > Paid time off (page 3)

You earn 1 hour of paid time off for every 30 hours worked. Your balance is capped at 80 hours. Ask for time off at least 14 days in advance so that your clients can be covered.

## Care rules > Medication (page 4)

Caregivers may remind a client to take medication and may open a container if the client asks. Caregivers may not administer medication, and may not decide a dose. Only a registered nurse may fill a weekly pill organizer.

## Care rules > Falls and other incidents (page 4)

If a client falls, call the on-call nurse right away, even if the client says they are fine. Do not lift a client who has fallen unless they are in immediate danger. A written incident report is due within 24 hours.

## Care rules > Training (page 4)

New caregivers complete an 8-hour orientation before their first solo visit. Every caregiver must complete 12 hours of in-service training each calendar year. Hours from approved workshops count toward this total.
