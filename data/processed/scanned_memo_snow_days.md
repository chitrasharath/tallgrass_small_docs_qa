## Snow day policy for this winter (page 1)

TALLGRASS HOME CARE MEMO

To: All caregivers and schedulers
From: Marcus Oyelaran, Operations Director
Date: November 3, 2025

Re: Snow day policy for this winter

When the office declares a Level 1 snow day, all visits go ahead as planned. Leave early and drive slowly.

When the office declares a Level 2 snow day, visits for clients on the essential list still go ahead. All other visits are cancelled. If your visit is cancelled, you are paid 2 hours of show-up pay for that visit.

The office posts the snow day level in the app by 5:30 a.m. If you are not sure, call the snow line at 555-0177.
